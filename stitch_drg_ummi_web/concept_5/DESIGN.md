## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intimate, welcoming, and reassuring atmosphere for a neighborhood family dental practice. The aesthetic departs sharply from clinical, sterile corporate medical portals, embracing instead the sensibility of a thoughtful apothecary, a beloved family home, or an inviting local studio.

The visual style blends **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Organic Tactile Comfort**:
- **Tone:** Empathetic, gentle, dignified, familial, rooted in Cibodas/Tangerang neighborhood warmth.
- **Atmosphere:** Anxiolytic and calming. Dental anxiety is mitigated through tender off-white foundations, delicate hairline boundaries, rich ink-like typography, and grounding clay accents.
- **Character:** Respects generational dental care—appealing equally to parents bringing toddlers for first cleanings and grandparents needing restorative attention.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around tactile earthen materials, ink on parchment, and purposeful contextual signaling:

- **Base Canvas (`#FAF6F0`):** Warm Ivory. Used across major viewport backgrounds to banish stark clinical hospital white.
- **Surface Layer (`#F4ECE1`):** Cream Container. Grounding tone for modular sections, gentle framing, service callouts, and schedule blocks.
- **Primary Typography & Anchors (`#152238`):** Deep Navy/Ink. Delivers authoritative contrast, high legibility, and architectural weight without the harshness of pure black.
- **Secondary Accent (`#C86D51`):** Muted Terracotta/Clay. Evokes artisanal ceramic warmth, human touch, and vitality; reserved for focus indicators, badges, highlights, and subtle interactive accents.
- **Tertiary Utility (`#25D366`):** WhatsApp Booking Green. Strictly reserved for direct patient communication, floating consultation buttons, and chat triage triggers. It is never used for general UI decorations.
- **Structural Border (`#E6DDD2`):** Warm Taupe. A whispering divider that defines structure through delicate hairlines rather than hard drop shadows.

## Typography

The pairing reconciles humanistic clinical rigor with literary poise:

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy (Newsreader):** Brings warmth, gentle cadence, and genuine gravitas. Italics should be used sparingly on key empathetic words (e.g., *lemah lembut*, *nyaman*, *ramah anak*).
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crafted with balanced counterforms and generous x-height, ensuring effortless readability for treatment descriptions, opening hours, instructions, and navigation across all screen densities.
- **Rule:** Never capitalize entire long headlines. Keep serifs natural, sentence-cased, and breathing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a human-scale, calm editorial flow rather than congested enterprise dashboards:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (max width: `1200px` to maintain focused reading length), consolidating to a 4-column layout on mobile.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Vertical rhythm relies on large open bays (`space-xl` and above) separating distinct treatment stories, doctor profiles, and clinic location guidance. Sections breathe generously to induce calmness.
- **Asymmetric Anchors:** Avoid rigid, repetitive boxes. Pair an editorial serif headline block on the left with an open, airy checklist or schedule block on the right.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid cold artificial interfaces, the system replaces synthetic drop shadows with **tactile surface layering and delicate borders**:

- **No Heavy Shadows:** Blown-out multi-stop black shadows are strictly prohibited. Depth is achieved via tonal juxtaposition: `#FAF6F0` (Ivory base) against `#F4ECE1` (Cream containers).
- **Hairline Framing:** Component borders use `1px solid #E6DDD2`. This imparts the feel of custom stationery and pressed cotton paper.
- **Soft Ambient Float:** When interactive surfaces lift on hover (e.g., patient guide cards, doctor credentials), apply only an ultra-subtle warm ambient aura: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(21, 34, 56, 0.05)`.
- **Active Focus:** Focus states use an inner soft ring of `#C86D51` at 25% opacity with an unyielding crisp 1px terracotta boundary.

## Shapes

A balanced `roundedness: 2` (base `8px`, containers `16px`, hero frames `24px`) keeps interactions soft, gentle, and child-safe without feeling overly bubbly or childish:

- **Interactive Nodes (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** Feature an 8px radius (`0.5rem`) for a tactile, friendly hand feel.
- **Content Panels & Quiet Pods:** Feature a 16px radius (`1rem`) paired with 1px `#E6DDD2` outlines.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action:** Uses full pill radius (`9999px`) to match the universal recognizable chat bubble paradigm.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Standard Clinic Inquiries):** Deep Navy (`#152238`) background with Warm Ivory (`#FAF6F0`) text. Hover introduces a warm subtle transition to `#223350`.
- **WhatsApp Appointment Action:** Strict pairing of WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`) with crisp white or deep navy text and official iconography. Features a gentle pulse or soft green ambient glow `rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.2)` on mobile viewports.
- **Secondary / Ghost Action:** Ivory surface with 1px `#E6DDD2` border and `#152238` text. Hover shifts background to `#F4ECE1`.

### Cards & Content Containers
- **Anti-Generic Card Rule:** Forbid sterile box-shadow cards with rounded corners centered on pure white backgrounds. Use cream canvas blocks (`#F4ECE1`) edged in `#E6DDD2` with unequal padding (e.g., generous vertical padding, lean horizontal framing) and asymmetrical typography hierarchy.
- **Doctor Introduction Block:** A warm editorial profile container pairing an authentic photograph framed by soft rounded curves, accompanied by doctor bio in Newsreader serif and direct credentials in muted clay badges.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background is always `#FAF6F0` with a 1px `#E6DDD2` border.
- Active states transition the border to `#C86D51` without abrupt outer glow halos.
- Placeholder copy uses friendly Indonesian neighborhood phrasing (e.g., *"Nama lengkap Ayah / Ibu / Kakak"*, *"Keluhan gigi atau jadwal yang diinginkan"*).

### Chips & Badges
- Category badges (e.g., *Konsultasi Anak*, *Tambal Estetik*, *Pembersihan Karang Gigi*) use `#F4ECE1` background, `#152238` text, and `#E6DDD2` borders.
- Urgent or active schedule indicators (e.g., *Buka Sore Ini*) use Terracotta accent text (`#C86D51`) inside a soft terracotta-tinted pill (`rgba(200, 109, 81, 0.1)`).

### List & Step Items
- Dental step-by-step guides (e.g., what to prepare before your visit) use custom terracotta circular numerals (`#C86D51`) over `#F4ECE1` disks, connected by hairline vertical rules.

### Location & Operational Timing Bar
- Dedicated grounded block detailing the Panunggangan Barat, Cibodas location with clear landmarks, integrated map snapshot framed in 1px warm taupe, and real-time open/closed indicators.
